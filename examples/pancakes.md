# Pancakes

Makes about 8 pancakes.

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups flour
- 3 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1 1/4 cups milk
- 1 egg
- 3 tablespoons melted butter

## Steps

1. Mix the flour, baking powder, sugar and salt in a large bowl.
2. Make a well in the centre. Pour in the milk, egg and melted butter.
3. Stir until just combined. A few lumps are fine.
4. Heat a lightly oiled pan over medium heat.
5. Pour about 1/4 cup of batter per pancake.
6. Flip when bubbles form on top, then cook until golden on the other side.
